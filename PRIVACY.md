# Wordly — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 17, 2026*

Wordly is a Chrome extension published by entringer.dev. This policy explains what the extension reads, what it stores, and what it does not do.

## In short

Wordly keeps everything on your device. It does not send your words, your searches or any other information to entringer.dev or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

## What Wordly reads

Wordly runs only on Google Search results pages (google.com and google.com.br). On those pages it reads your search and the content of the results page, for one purpose: to detect whether the page shows the definition of a word. This happens entirely inside your browser. Nothing read on the page is stored unless you click the save button.

## What Wordly stores

When you click save, Wordly stores the following in your browser's local extension storage:

- the word;
- the definition shown on the page, up to 500 characters;
- the language of the definition;
- the date and time you saved it;
- a technical identifier of how the definition was detected, used only to diagnose detection problems.

Wordly does not store the address of the page, your search history, or any other content from the page. Your saved words are not synced between devices.

## What Wordly does not do

- It does not transmit any data off your device. Wordly makes no network requests.
- It does not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
- It does not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties.
- It does not use your data for advertising, profiling or determining creditworthiness.
- It does not run on any website other than Google Search results pages.

## Your control over your data

You can delete any saved word from the Wordly popup at any time. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all the words it stored. Because your data never leaves your device, entringer.dev has no copy of it and cannot access, restore or delete it on your behalf.

## Limited Use

The use of information obtained by Wordly complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Wordly changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is published. Any feature that sends data off your device will be off by default and will require your explicit consent.
